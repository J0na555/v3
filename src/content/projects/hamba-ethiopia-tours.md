## Client context

Hamba Ethiopia Tours is a tourism platform for Ethiopia-based tours, connecting travelers with curated itineraries across the country. The brief called for a premium brand presence: distinctive UI/UX design, multi-language support, and a custom tour booking experience.

## My role

Frontend development focused on the customer-facing side — responsive design, interactive animations, and conversion-focused CTAs that move visitors from browsing to booking. I worked with Cloudinary on image and performance optimization so destination pages stay fast on any device.

## Result

A polished platform with professional branding and an advanced SEO & GEO setup that supports organic discovery. The site gives travelers a premium first impression and gives Hamba a direct path from research to booking.
