## Client context

Ethio Origins Tour is a tourism platform for Ethiopia-based tour operators, built around destination-first discovery. The site pairs editorial photography with immersive destination pages so travelers can explore itineraries before booking a consultation.

## My role

Frontend development focused on premium UI/UX — a destination-first design system, multi-language support, and conversion-focused CTAs that guide visitors toward a consultation. I shipped it on a high-performance Next.js architecture with Tailwind CSS and Framer Motion, and handled professional branding and logo design alongside image and performance optimization via Cloudinary.

## Result

A fast, SEO-optimized platform with a clean booking flow and a consistent brand across every touchpoint. The site gives Ethio Origins a credible destination brand for both travelers and the operators it serves.
